Current Shortage Guidance by Resource
LPG
Book immediately via IOCL / Ujjwala app — cylinder availability is projected to tighten in 72 hours across eastern UP, Bihar, and Manipur. Do not wait for existing cylinder to run out.
Water
Reservoir levels in Rajasthan and Bihar are below 40%. Store 3–5 days of drinking water if you are in a flagged district. Municipal tanker schedules are posted at your local ward office.
Food Grains
Ration card holders should collect the full monthly allocation now. FCI depots in Bihar and Jharkhand have 18-day buffer — procurement is underway to restore 30-day minimum.
Medicines
Jan Aushadhi Kendras in Jharkhand tribal belt are reporting low generic drug stocks. Contact your district CMHO office for referrals to alternative dispensaries.
01
Check your state's supply status
Use the state selector above. Critical means active shortage; warning means act before it worsens. Check each resource individually — a state may be critical on LPG but stable on food.
02
Locate your nearest distribution point
LPG: your registered dealer. Water: municipal office or ward tanker schedule. Food: PDS ration shop or FCI depot. Fuel: any OMC outlet. Medicines: Jan Aushadhi Kendra or government hospital pharmacy.
03
Register and book in advance
LPG: use Umang app, IOCL app, or call 1800-233-3555. Ration: ensure your Aadhaar is linked at the nearest CSC. Water tankers: register via your municipal corporation's portal or their helpline.
04
Conserve — do not hoard
Panic buying worsens shortages for everyone in your area. Report hoarding or black market pricing to your district supply officer or the NFSA grievance portal at 1967.
05
Stay updated via official channels
Subscribe to NDMA alerts via Aarogya Setu or SMS 7738299899. This dashboard is updated every 30 minutes from government API sources. Your district collector's office publishes daily supply bulletins.
